# 指南要点 Guidelines (summary only)

Only these two, with versions. They are general guidance, not personal advice. **以医生为准 — the doctor decides.** Never present them as a diagnosis or as an instruction to skip a follow-up.

## Fleischner Society 2017 (incidentally found pulmonary nodules, adults 35+, not for lung cancer screening, not for known cancer or immunosuppression)

Solid nodules, single:
- under 6 mm: low-risk usually no routine follow-up; high-risk optional CT at 12 months.
- 6 to 8 mm: low-risk CT at 6 to 12 months, then consider 18 to 24 months; high-risk CT at 6 to 12 months, then 18 to 24 months.
- over 8 mm: consider CT at 3 months, PET/CT, or tissue sampling.

Solid nodules, multiple:
- under 6 mm: low-risk none; high-risk optional CT at 12 months.
- 6 mm or more: CT at 3 to 6 months, then 18 to 24 months (low-risk) or 2 and 4 years (high-risk).

Subsolid, single:
- ground-glass under 6 mm: no routine follow-up.
- ground-glass 6 mm or more: CT at 6 to 12 months, then every 2 years until 5 years.
- part-solid under 6 mm: no routine follow-up.
- part-solid 6 mm or more: CT at 3 to 6 months; if unchanged and solid part under 6 mm, yearly for 5 years; solid part 6 mm or more: consider PET/CT or resection.

Multiple subsolid: under 6 mm CT at 3 to 6 months, then consider 2 and 4 years; 6 mm or more, follow the dominant nodule.

Sizes are the average of long and short axis, in mm. Risk factors: age, smoking, family history, upper-lobe location, spiculation, emphysema, fibrosis.

## Lung-RADS v2022 (lung cancer screening CT, low-dose, ages in screening program)

- 0: incomplete or prior CT needed.
- 1: no nodules, or nodules with benign features: continue annual screening.
- 2: benign appearance or behaviour (solid under 6 mm, ground-glass under 30 mm, etc.): annual screening.
- 3: probably benign (solid 6 to under 8 mm, part-solid under 6 mm total, ground-glass 30 mm or more): CT in 6 months.
- 4A: suspicious (solid 8 to under 15 mm, part-solid 6 mm or more with solid part 6 to under 8 mm, growing): CT in 3 months, PET/CT may be considered.
- 4B: very suspicious (solid 15 mm or more, solid part 8 mm or more, new or growing): diagnostic workup by the doctor.
- 4X: category 3 or 4 with additional suspicious features.
- S modifier: significant other findings.

Lung-RADS applies to screening programs only; a report that gives a category already reflects the radiologist's judgement. Read the exact current ACR tables for anything beyond this summary.
